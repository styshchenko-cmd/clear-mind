# ClearMind privacy policy

ClearMind is a browser extension that blocks, time-limits and tidies the sites you
choose. It has no servers and makes no network requests.

**What it handles.** To apply your rules it reads the address of the tab you are on.
For the sites on your lists and the categories you time-limit, it records the domain,
time spent per day, and how often a block page was shown or a site was unlocked.
It also stores your rules and a salted hash of your unblock password (never the
password itself).

**Where it goes.** Nowhere. Everything stays in your browser's extension storage on
this device. Nothing is sent, sold, shared or used for advertising, and there is no
analytics or tracking.

**How long.** Daily statistics are kept for 400 days. You can erase them at any time in
*settings → reset statistics*. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its data.

**Contact.** Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/styshchenko-cmd/clear-mind/issues
